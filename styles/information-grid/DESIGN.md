# Information Grid

Information Grid is a cool laboratory visual language for information diagrams,
uncertainty, binary choices, and measured data. Midnight blue surfaces carry a
thin grid; electric mint marks encoded signals; pale blue labels explain the
structure; monospace metadata makes measurements feel instrumented.

## Direction

- Prefer binary cells, branching diagrams, probability marks, and measured data motion.
- Keep hierarchy clear: the signal or relationship is the hero, with labels supporting it.
- Use the grid as structure and continuity, not as decorative noise.

## Anti-patterns

- Poster typography or generic title-card lockups.
- Vermilion or warm editorial accent systems.
- Dense texture that competes with the diagram.
- Unmotivated camera movement or bouncy transitions.
