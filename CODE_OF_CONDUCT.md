# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and
welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Accept documented project decisions and revise proposals when new evidence
  appears.
- Report concerns privately when public discussion would expose someone to
  harm.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report a concern to the repository maintainers through the private contact
listed in `SECURITY.md`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept
confidential where possible.
